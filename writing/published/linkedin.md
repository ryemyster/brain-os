# About

I run platform programs that have stalled. Most PMs would manage them. I diagnose them. They do not usually fail on execution. They fail on governance, decision rights and a narrative that has replaced diagnosis. I find where the system broke and fix that first. I can do this because I read code, review PRs and challenge architecture from inside the system instead of above it. That is leverage, not delivery work.

I own platform direction and not just delivery. My work is figuring out what the platform should become and then rewriting the roadmap to get there.
