# Achievements & Stories

Wins, metrics, and STAR-format stories for interviews. Add one section per achievement. BrainOS `prep` reads this to surface relevant stories for each company.

## Template (copy for each entry)

**Role:** [Title at Company, Year]
**Situation:** [Brief context — what was happening, what was broken or missing]
**Task:** [What you were specifically responsible for]
**Action:** [What you did — be concrete, use "I" not "we"]
**Result:** [Quantified outcome — %, $, time saved, users affected]
**Keywords:** [2-4 themes: e.g. growth, AI, 0→1, cross-functional, FinTech]
