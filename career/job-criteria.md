## Must-haves

- Real product ownership with clear decision rights (not coordination layer)
- Platform or system-level problem (not feature roadmap execution)
- Engineering has real influence on roadmap and architecture decisions
- Measurable outcomes tied to product decisions (not activity metrics)
- AI is core to system design, workflows, or infrastructure (not bolt-on feature)
- Cross-functional integration across product, engineering, and GTM treated as core work
- Access to decision-makers and ability to influence system-level changes

---

## Nice-to-haves

- 0→1 product building combined with scaling experience
- Company has a data, platform, or network-effect moat
- Leadership with strong product culture (not purely sales-led)
- Investment in AI workflows (evaluation systems, infra, not just demos)
- Opportunity to fix or redesign operating models and system constraints

---

## Hard nos

- Feature PM roles with no system ownership
- Roles focused on roadmap coordination, intake, or reporting
- “AI PM” roles that are prompt/UI wrappers without infra or workflow depth
- Process-heavy orgs (SAFe theater, excessive ceremony, unclear ownership)
- Sales-led organizations where product is downstream of GTM
- Strategy or innovation roles without execution authority
- Contract roles significantly below market with no path to ownership
- Cultures with:
  - unclear ownership
  - metrics disconnected from outcomes
  - alignment theater instead of decision-making
  - high coordination overhead without output

  - re-establish decision rights and ownership

  - realign systems to produce measurable outcomes
- Track record of impact:
  - ~$60K/month coordination cost removed through governance redesign
  - $500K platform funding secured via product strategy
  - 10x user growth in 0→1 product build
- Experience across security, media/streaming, healthcare, and AI systems with consistent pattern recognition
- Founder experience (Ascendvent, AOSI) with validated pivots and demand testing
- Technical credibility from engineering background; operates inside system constraints
- Strong bias toward outcomes over activity, clarity over process, and ownership over ambiguity
