# CPO / PM Leader Hiring Signals (Field Intel)

What actually moves hiring managers and CPOs right now — not job descriptions, but what they say out loud.

---

## Gil (CPO-level signal — June 2026)

**The bottleneck is knowing what to build, not building it.**
His team is quick to prototype and slow to ship. They will kill a feature close to the finish line if customers aren't using it.

**Workflow: vibe-code → feel → spec**
1. Vibe-code the feature first
2. Feel it in the product
3. Reverse the working code back into a written spec
4. Condense to **3 sentences for execs**, expand to every edge case for engineers
5. That spec becomes the artifact everyone works off

**On hiring:**
Has stopped caring how many AI tools you can name. Wants **taste** and a **real point of view on where things are going**.

---

## What this means going into interviews

- Lead with **judgment**, not tooling literacy
- Show you have a mental model for what's worth building and why
- Come with a POV on AI's direction that isn't just "it's changing everything"
- Be ready to demonstrate taste — bring a real opinion, even a contrarian one
- Customer usage/signal > feature completeness — frame decisions around this
