## Restore delivery on stalled SIEM program

**Role:** Senior Product Manager, Bank of America (2025–Present)  
**Situation:** SIEM modernization program stalled across security ops, engineering, and compliance. No clear ownership, roadmap drift, and delivery commitments unreliable.  
**Task:** Re-establish delivery by fixing governance and aligning system-level execution.  
**Action:** Defined decision rights across orgs, separated detection research from production workflows, rebuilt operating model, and aligned a milestone-driven migration plan across Splunk and Anvilogic.  
**Result:** Eliminated ~$60K/month in coordination overhead, restored predictable delivery, and re-enabled roadmap commitments across three orgs.  
**Keywords:** platform, governance, system design, enterprise
