## Job Description — Lead Product Manager, Professional Services

**The Role:**
Operates at the intersection of product strategy, customer need, and commercial opportunity — shaping solutions that extend the product portfolio and support strategic customer outcomes.

**Core Responsibilities:**
- Define and drive product approach for PS-led opportunities
- Identify where customer needs can be solved through scalable product extensions, configuration, or targeted enhancements
- Evaluate opportunities through customer value, strategic fit, implementation complexity, and long-term maintainability
- Translate customer-driven requests into clear product decisions, recommendations, and next steps
- Partner across Product, Engineering, Sales, CS, and Professional Services
- Use first-principles thinking to break down loosely defined asks into clear problem statements
- Distinguish between one-off customer requests, repeatable product patterns, and strategic platform investments
- Drive work from opportunity sizing through delivery, launch, and iteration
- Bring data fluency to reporting, analytics, and customer-specific insight requests
- Help build a repeatable PS product model that balances custom work with scalable investment

**Requirements:**
- 6–10 years PM experience
- Strong B2B SaaS, platform, or operational software background
- Track record of solving ambiguous, cross-functional product problems
- Experience partnering with customer-facing teams (Sales, CS, PS) on strategic opportunities
- Strong data fluency — custom reporting, analytics-heavy workflows
- High-ownership mindset, bias for action
- Consumer PM background earlier in career + B2B more recently is a plus
