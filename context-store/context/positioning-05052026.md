# Ryan McDonald — Role Narrative & Positioning

This document bridges internal clarity (Mnookin) and market reality (PM DNA + Market Fit).  
Use this for interviews, recruiter conversations, and positioning.

---

## Core Positioning (use this everywhere)

Senior platform PM who restores delivery when systems stall.

I come in when:

- ownership is unclear  
- outcomes are not measured  
- roadmap and architecture have drifted apart  

Most teams treat this as an execution problem. It is usually a system problem.

I identify where the system broke, fix decision rights and operating model, then re-sequence the roadmap so teams can ship again.

---

## What I Actually Do (translated for market)

I do not just manage roadmaps. I fix the system that produces them.

That means:

- Defining decision rights across orgs  
- Aligning architecture and product strategy  
- Removing coordination overhead  
- Replacing activity metrics with outcome metrics  
- Creating operating models that engineering can actually execute against  

This is why I get pulled into stalled or high-risk programs.

---

## Proof (anchor points)

- ~$60K/month coordination cost removed (Bank of America)  
- $500K platform funding secured (Splunk)  
- 12% adoption lift across 2,000+ customers (Splunk)  
- $5M enterprise revenue retained during acquisition (Edgecast)  
- 50M tests/month scaled in 6 weeks (Cognizant)  

Pattern: system fix → execution follows

---

## How I Think (what makes me different)

- Most execution problems are system failures  
- AI amplifies system flaws before it creates advantage  
- Product management is a decision system, not a roadmap function  
- Activity is not progress. Output is not outcome  
- Governance is a tool. Poorly designed governance creates hidden cost  

---

## What I Am Actually Looking For

A role where:

- the problem is structural, not just execution  
- engineering has real influence on decisions  
- product owns outcomes, not just coordination  
- I can fix system-level issues, not just ship features  

Titles that usually map:

- Senior PM (Platform)  
- Staff PM (Platform / Systems)  
- Select Director roles with real ownership  

---

## Where I Create the Most Leverage

- Stalled platform programs  
- Post-acquisition or reorg environments  
- Fragmented systems with unclear ownership  
- Products with strong engineering teams but weak product structure  

---

## What I Avoid

- Feature PM roles with no system ownership  
- Process-heavy orgs with no decision authority  
- AI roles that are UI wrappers with no workflow depth  
- Sales-led roadmap environments  
- Roles where responsibility exists without authority  

---

## Internal Truth (do not lead with this, but use to guide decisions)

I am naturally oriented toward:

- Chief of Staff / Strategic Ops / Special Projects work  
- Cross-org problem solving with executive proximity  
- Fixing messy systems with no clear owner  

Senior PM roles are currently the **vehicle**, not always the end state.
